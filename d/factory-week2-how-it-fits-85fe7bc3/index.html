01

Three parts, one direction

built · verified 2026-09-16

The browser talks only to the portal and the web service. Only the web service talks to Salesforce, always as one integration user. Nothing in the portal or the browser holds business data.

Three parts. The portal is static and stateless. The web service is the only thing holding a Salesforce credential. Operators never touch the portal; their work is a tab and a flow inside Salesforce.

02

Every call runs the same pipeline

built

There is no special path. Anonymous requests, partner requests, staff requests and agent requests all go through the same six steps, and every one of them ends in an audit row, including refusals.

Two identities carry a credential the browser never sees: staff and agent bearer keys. The partner identity is a signed cookie. A bearer key wins over a cookie, and a bad credential never falls back to another identity.

03

A partner applies, Factory approves

built · in the demo

Week 2's first story. The portal creates the records; the operator decides inside Salesforce; a flow sends the invite and writes the audit row.

Approving is a Salesforce action, never a portal one. The decision flow and both quick actions require a primary contact before Approve, so an approval can never go out without someone to invite.

04

Signing in without a password

built · in the demo

Week 2's second story. An email address, one link, one use, fifteen minutes. The reply is the same for every address, so the form reveals nothing about who is a partner.

The same link a second time is refused: the hash is cleared on first use. The cookie carries the Contact and Account ids, and the web service re-reads both records before serving any session, so a partner set to Inactive in Salesforce loses access on the next call.

06

What lives in Salesforce

sandbox only

Everything the portal owns is ours and named with a Portal or Partner prefix. Factory's own objects, fields, layouts and flows are read where needed and never changed.

Partner_Influence__c, Factory's junction for deal registrations, carries our registration fields from Week 1 and comes into play in Week 4. It is left out here on purpose.
