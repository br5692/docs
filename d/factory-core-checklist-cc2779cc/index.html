Core check, weeks 1 and 2

Under 10 minutes. It follows the order of the 2026-09-23 client demo, so one pass is also the rehearsal. If a step does not match "Expect", stop and report it.

Before you start (1 min)

Open
Wake the service: tool layer health. Expect "ok":true and "sf":"ok". The first call can take about 20 seconds.
Tabs: Apply, Sign in, the Partner Review app (App Launcher), and Portal Audit Log, All in Salesforce. Keep your inbox open.
Use a fresh company name and website each run, and email addresses you can receive (plus addressing works).

The path (about 8 min)

#DoExpectTime
1 Apply as a new company. Use an email whose domain differs from the website, so the mismatch shows. Application received page. In Salesforce the new Account's application note reads "Email domain mismatch: ... does not match website host ...".1 min
2 Partner Review, Pending, Companies: find the row, check the primary contact, click Approve, then the solid Approve. The row leaves Pending. Decided, Companies shows it Approved with the time in Pacific.1 min
3 Inbox for that address. The approval (invite) email arrives.½ min
4 Sign in with that address and open the link. The link arrives and opens the dashboard with the company name top right.1 min
5 Same company, second person: Apply with the same website and a new address. Then request a sign-in link for the new address. The application succeeds. Sign-in shows the usual neutral message and no email arrives.1 min
6 Partner Review, Pending, People: Approve that person, confirm. Request a sign-in link again. This time the link arrives and signs them in.1½ min
7 Deny path: Apply as another new company, then Deny it in Partner Review with a note. The row moves to Decided as Denied, and the denial email arrives.1 min
8 Portal Audit Log, All, newest first. One row for every step above. The company approval writes two rows (invite on the Account, access on the contact). Step 5 shows sign-in Denied, no_approved_contact.1 min
Good to know for the call
  • The domain-mismatch warning is on the Account record's application note. The Partner Review app does not show it, so open the Account for question OQ-23.
  • Step 5 is the one path nobody has watched work until now. If it lets the second person straight in, stop and report it tonight.
  • Right before the call: wake the health page again and move the audit cutoff to the current minute.
