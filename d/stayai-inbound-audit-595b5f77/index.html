01

Routing

broken

The risk raised on the call: someone asks to talk to sales, and nobody follows up.

There is no active lead routing. One assignment rule exists in the entire org, New Partner Assignment, and it is inactive. The org has no queues at all, so every lead is owned by an individual from the moment it is created.

130 leads have sat in status New for more than 90 days. 115 of them are owned by two deactivated users — Michael Yates (79, Leadership) and Amir Torabi (36, Partnerships). Both are IsActive = false. Those leads cannot be followed up, because their owners cannot log in.

Counting leads created before the window as well, 251 leads in status New are owned by five deactivated users.

26 of the 208 had a task logged before the opportunity existed; they count as an immediate touch.

On the Lead object, 182 of 226 leads (81%) have no logged task ever. Of the 44 that were worked, the median response was 30 minutes. Leads that get picked up get picked up fast. Most are never picked up.

Demo booking is healthy. Median time from opportunity created to demo is 2 days, and 64 of 69 land inside a week. Only 69 of the 213 carry a demo date, all on deals created from March 2026 on, so this reads the last six months rather than the full year.

03

Lead scoring

stopped

Scoring used to run, and stopped in mid-2024.

  • Pardot is installed with pi__score__c and pi__Pardot_Last_Scored_At__c. None of the 226 leads and none of the 7,009 contacts created in the window carry a score.
  • 322 older contacts carry a non-zero score, all created between January 2023 and June 2024. No contact created after 8 July 2024 has a score value at all, which dates when scoring stopped.
  • The standard Rating field is empty on all 226 leads, so there is no hot-lead flag.
  • LeadSource is blank on 220 of 226 leads, so lead-level attribution is unusable.
04

Demo request path

partly instrumented

294 Chili Piper demos booked in the window.

  • 95 attached to an Opportunity, 166 attached to an Account, 33 attached to neither.
  • 22 cancelled.
  • No-show reads zero across all 294 events. The field is not being written, rather than nobody missing a demo. Cancelled and no-show demos are the cheapest re-engagement list a team has, and half of it is invisible.

Separately, 46 of the 213 inbound opportunities sit on an account with no segment at all.

One limit worth stating. Whether every web form submission becomes a record at all cannot be answered from the CRM alone. It needs the form tool and the website analytics alongside it. Everything above measures what reached Salesforce, not what was sent.

Partner carries 40% of all won revenue at the best win rate of any volume channel, and it is the fastest-growing: 145 opportunities created in 2025 against 204 so far in 2026. Marketing went the other way, 167 in 2025 against 111 so far in 2026. Most partner deals do name the partner. The Partner lookup is filled on 168 of the 204 (82%). The gap is the other 36, plus nothing that makes the field required. Events is the weakest channel at 22%, 13 wins against 47 losses, and it costs money to attend.

The CX figure is an artifact rather than channel strength. Those deals are only entered once they are known to close.

06

What to fix

The first three answer the routing risk directly. None require new tooling.

  • Reassign the 251 New leads owned by deactivated users, 115 of them from the last 12 months. Same-day fix, no build.
  • Turn on lead routing. A queue plus an active assignment rule, so no lead is ever owned by nobody.
  • Set a follow-up expectation on inbound. 46 opportunities waited more than a week.
  • Decide on Pardot scoring. Switch it back on and route hot leads first, or retire it.
  • Fix the no-show field so cancelled and missed demos become a re-engagement list.
  • Close the partner gap. Make Partner required when the deal source is Partner, fill the 36 blanks, and build the partner performance view on it. Agreed on the 09-22 call as low hanging fruit.
