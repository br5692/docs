01

The red flag

real, and measurable

The risk raised on the call: someone asks to talk to sales, and nobody follows up.

372 people outside Stay submitted the website Book Demo form (387 submissions). When someone submits and does not book a slot, HubSpot logs a Demo Not Scheduled task about 30 minutes later. 92 people got that flag.

What happened after the flag (92 people)Count
Booked a meeting later19
Got a rep email or call (mostly Outreach sequence emails)45

The first two rows overlap by 19 people. Where a rep did reach out, the median wait was 43.5 hours, and 20 of the 45 heard back within a day.

Of the 47 with no follow-up on record, 16 are business prospects with a company email address. The other 31 used a personal address (28), sit on a partner account, or had a rep work a colleague at the same company.

23 of the 92 are owned by a deactivated user, including all 14 leads among the 47. Nobody is assigned to call them back.

02

Inbound volume

marketing falling

How much comes in, by the measures the CRM holds.

  • 372 people outside Stay asked for a demo through the website form (387 submissions).
  • 125 demos booked through the Chili Piper demo router since March 2026.
  • 213 opportunities created with a Marketing or Events deal source.
  • 226 lead records created. The Lead object is lightly used; 871 of the 892 form submissions logged in the window sit on contacts, not leads.

Marketing-sourced opportunities ran 38 to 46 a quarter through 2025, 50 in Q1 2026 and 40 in Q2, then 21 in Q3 to 22 September.

03

Routing

demos yes, leads no

The demo booking path is routed. Record ownership in Salesforce is not.

Demo requests are routed by Chili Piper. The NEW - Book a Demo router has booked 125 demos since its first booking in March 2026, every one scheduled by the prospect. The median wait from booking to meeting is 2.1 days, and 99 of the 112 not cancelled were inside a week. 13 were cancelled.

Salesforce lead assignment is switched off. One assignment rule exists, New Partner Assignment, and it is inactive. The org has no queues. Unless someone changes it by hand, a record keeps the owner it was created with, even after that person leaves.

That is how 250 leads in status New ended up owned by five deactivated users, mostly Michael Yates and Gio Demaio. Only 17 of them show a website form submission, so most are not inbound requests, but nobody can work any of them while they sit there.

04

Speed to lead

slow on the gap

Booking is fast. The response to people who do not book is slow.

  • Prospects who book wait a median of 2.1 days for the meeting (section 03).
  • Prospects who do not book wait a median of 43.5 hours for a rep, when one reaches out at all (section 01).
  • 135 of the 226 leads created in the window have no task or meeting logged at all.

What this does not use. The first activity logged on an inbound opportunity is usually a Gong recording of the call itself (123 of 208) or the prospect's own email (26), so it cannot show rep response time. Speed is measured on the demo request list instead, where the start point is known.

05

Lead scoring

stopped

Scoring used to run, and stopped in mid-2024.

  • Pardot is installed with pi__score__c and pi__Pardot_Last_Scored_At__c. None of the 226 leads and none of the 6,997 contacts created in the window carry a score.
  • 322 older contacts carry a non-zero score, all created between January 2023 and June 2024. No contact created after 8 July 2024 has a score value at all.
  • The standard Rating field is empty on all 226 leads, so there is no hot-lead flag.
  • LeadSource is blank on 220 of 226 leads, so lead-level attribution is unusable.
06

Tracking gaps

partly instrumented

What the CRM cannot see today.

  • 46 of the 213 inbound opportunities (Marketing and Events deal sources) sit on an account with no segment.
  • Form capture cannot be checked from the CRM. Whether every web form submission becomes a record needs the form tool and the website analytics alongside it. Everything here measures what reached Salesforce.

The table comes from a grouped aggregate. Recomputing it row by row from all 592 raw closed records produces identical counts, rates and totals.

Partner carries 40% of all won revenue at the best win rate of any volume channel, and it is the fastest-growing: 145 opportunities created in 2025 against 204 so far in 2026. Marketing went the other way, 167 in 2025 against 111 so far in 2026. Most partner deals name the partner. The Partner lookup is filled on 168 of the 204 (82%). The gap is the other 36, and nothing makes the field required. Events is the weakest channel at 22%, 13 wins against 47 losses, and it costs money to attend.

The CX figure is an artifact rather than channel strength. Those deals are only entered once they are known to close.

08

What to fix

The first three answer the red flag directly. None require new tooling.

  • Give the Demo Not Scheduled list an owner and a one-business-day response. Today the median is 43.5 hours and 47 of 92 have nothing on record.
  • Reassign records owned by deactivated users, starting with the 23 demo requesters, then the 250 New leads.
  • Turn on lead assignment. A queue plus an active rule, so a new record never lands on someone who has left.
  • Decide on Pardot scoring. Switch it back on and route hot leads first, or retire it.
  • Close the partner gap. Make Partner required when the deal source is Partner, fill the 36 blanks, and build the partner performance view on it.
