Run top to bottom on the hosted stack. Each row: what to do, what should happen, what it proves, where to confirm. Anything off: reply with the step number and what you saw.

Setup

  • Portal, Weeks 1 and 2: https://factory-partner-portal.pages.dev. Portal, Weeks 3 to 5: https://latest.factory-partner-portal.pages.dev.
  • Salesforce sandbox: https://factoryai--partial.sandbox.my.salesforce.com, app Factory Sales, tab Partner Applications. Admin login from the handoff. Setup pages used below: Setup, Users, Setup, Certificate and Key Management, App Manager (the portal's External Client App), Setup, Object Manager, Account, Portal Audit Log, All, Setup, Custom Settings.
  • Test users, already in the sandbox, reached by Login As from Setup, Users: [TEST] Operator (Portal Admin Fields permission set) and [TEST] No Review (no portal permissions).
  • Partner side: a fictional company and an email address you own.
  • Emails, pick one path before starting. Path A, real inbox: sandbox Setup, Email, Deliverability, Access level All email for the session, back to System email only after; the hosted mail transport is set to Salesforce for the session in the handoff. Path B, no email: read each link from the hosted service log (Render, service factory-partner-portal-tool-layer, Logs, search the address).

Automated suites on another machine

Read access to https://github.com/br5692/factory-partner-portal from the handoff, git, Node 22 or newer, pnpm 10.12.4. No secrets.

Week 5: Deal decisions

You are the operator with the decision permission, then the partner. Preview address https://latest.factory-partner-portal.pages.dev. Fifteen minutes.

#DoExpectProvesConfirm where
6.1As an operator without Portal Deal Decision, open Partner Applications.No Partner Registration Decision box and no Decided card.Decisions are hidden without the permission.Salesforce
6.2As an operator with the permission, open Partner Applications.Registration Decision box with a Registration dropdown of pending deals; a Registrations Decided card.The permission reveals the decision tools.Salesforce
6.3Pick your registration, choose Deny, leave the note empty, Next.Note Required screen. Nothing changes on the registration.Deny and Needs Information require a reason.Salesforce
6.4Pick it again, choose Approve, add a note, Next.Registration Status Approved, Decided By and Decided At stamped, note saved. It moves from Pending to Decided.Approval stamps who, when and why.Salesforce, Partner Influence
6.5Register a second deal for the same customer as a second partner of the same type, then try to Approve it.Approval Blocked screen with one sentence. Nothing written.One primary partner per deal, enforced at approval.Salesforce
6.6Register another deal as the first partner. Pick it, choose Needs Information, add a question, Next.Status Needs Information, note saved. It stays in the pending list.Needs Information sends it back without deciding.Salesforce
6.7Portal Audit Log, All, newest rows.registration_decision rows for each outcome with the deciding user as actor; a decision email row per decision (Error email_send_failed unless deliverability is on).Every decision and email attempt is audited.Salesforce
6.8Path A only: check the partner inbox.Approved and Needs Information emails from the portal sender address, each with the note.Partners are told the decision and why.Inbox
6.9Sign in as the first partner. Open the dashboard.Four tiles above the list: approved registrations, approved value, awaiting decision, approval rate. Numbers match your deals.Partners see their own numbers, nobody else's.Browser
6.10Open the approved registration.Decision block: Approved, the note, the decided date. No name of who decided.Partner sees the decision, never the decider.Browser
6.11Open the Needs Information registration. Type a reply and Send.The reply appears under the decision with its time. Status returns to Pending so the AE decides again.Partner answers in place and the deal goes back to the AE.Browser
6.12Open that Partner Influence record in Salesforce.The reply shows in the Partner Portal section of the record and on the decision screen for that row; a registration_reply audit row exists.The operator sees the partner's answer.Salesforce
6.13Setup, Custom Settings, Portal Settings.Decision Manager Copy unchecked, no rows.Manager copies are off until Factory turns them on.Salesforce Setup
6.14Open the main address https://factory-partner-portal.pages.dev, not the preview, signed in as the partner.No tiles, no decision block, no reply box. Dashboard as in Week 2.Week 5 stays hidden on the public address.Browser
