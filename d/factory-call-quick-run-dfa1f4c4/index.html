Quick run, Week 2 review and Week 3

The core checks to walk through together on the call: the Week 2 review changes, then the Week 3 invite, DocuSign signing and signed agreement. About 25 minutes. One contract send uses one DocuSign send. Each row: what to do, what should happen, what it proves, where to confirm. Anything off: reply with the step number and what you saw.

00

Setup

  • Portal: https://factory-partner-portal.pages.dev (runs the Week 3 build).
  • Wake the service first: health check. Expect "ok":true and "sf":"ok". The first call can take about 20 seconds.
  • Keep three tabs open: the Partner Review app, Portal Audit Log, All, and your Domestique inbox.
  • Use a fresh company name and website for each new company, and a Domestique address with a plus tag (blair.purdy+<tag>@domestique.info).
  • Partner Review Week is set to 3. Screenshots of Partner Review were taken before the Joined via column replaced Source and Status; the steps describe the current screen.

Week 3: invite and contract

Operator, then the invited partner, then Salesforce. Ten minutes.

#DoExpectProvesConfirm where
2.1Invite and send, held page. In Partner Review click Invite partner, fill it in with the invite bookmarklet, leave Send the contract now ticked and click Invite and send contract. Then open the invite email and follow its sign-in line and the sign-in link that arrives.A green toast names the company and contact; the row shows Invitation, Approved, Contract Sent (the first send after a quiet spell can take about 30 seconds). Sign it from that email."One step creates the partner and sends invite and contract; an unsigned partner is held on the Contract page.Partner Review, Decided; browser; inbox

The signed email arrives within about 30 seconds. Its link lands on the dashboard, where the contract card shows Signed and View signed agreement opens the signed PDF.DocuSign emails the partner directly, the result reaches Salesforce on its own, and the partner can open the signed agreement.DocuSign; inbox; browser

Open that company's Account, then Partner Review, Decided, and find Fernbridge Group.Account: Contract Status Signed; Partner Accepted, Contract Sent and Contract Signed At in order; Partner Lifecycle Status 3.
