03Staff approve and send the agreement3–9 min

  1. In Partner Review, open the application and show the conflict check.
  2. Click Approve. Show the email preview, then send.
  3. Click Send contract. Sign from DocuSign's email.
  4. If DocuSign is slow, use Mark Contract Signed on the Account in Salesforce.
  5. Show the Account at Signed.

04The partner signs in9–11 min

Sign-in pagehttps://factory-partner-portal.pages.dev/sign-in
  1. Type the new partner email and check it before submitting.
  2. Click the link in the sign-in email (valid 15 minutes).
  3. The dashboard opens. It is nearly empty for a new partner.

06Staff review and approve the deal14–18 min

  1. In Partner Review, open the deal.
  2. Optional: choose Needs information and send a question.
  3. Click Approve.
  4. Open the new Opportunity on the customer Account.
Sample questionWhat is the expected close date and who is the decision maker?
