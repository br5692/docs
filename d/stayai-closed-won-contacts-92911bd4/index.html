02What changed

New flow. Starts when a deal becomes Closed Won. Runs in the background after the save, so it does not block the rep. Finds the campaign by its Campaign Key.
New flow, continued. Collects the primary, attribution, champion, economic buyer, influencer and contact role contacts. Skips contacts already in the campaign.
Before (v2, same as production). Last Touch flow has no entry condition. Every campaign add changes the contact's Last Touch Campaign.
After (v3). One entry condition: skip campaigns with Exclude From Last Touch ticked. All other campaigns work as before.
