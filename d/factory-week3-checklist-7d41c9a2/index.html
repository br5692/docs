Core check, Week 3

About 15 minutes on the live site. It covers the Week 3 items (e-signature and status sync, pre-approved invite, timestamps) and the changes from the Week 2 review. If a step does not match "Expect", stop and report it.

Before you start (1 min)

  • Wake the service: tool layer health. Expect "ok":true and "sf":"ok". The first call can take about 20 seconds.
  • Tabs: Apply, the Partner Review app, and Portal Audit Log, All in Salesforce. Keep the Domestique inbox open.
  • Use a fresh company name and website each run.
