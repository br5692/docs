Account Status Guide

What's new in Salesforce for working prospect accounts, and how to use it. Based on the Salesforce Cleanup Brief and the 10/15 review call.

1. At a glance

  • Every prospect account has a clear status: Unworked, Hot Lead, Target Account, Working, In Opportunity, Nurture, Cooldown, Closed Lost or Disqualified.
  • Salesforce sets Working, In Opportunity, Closed Lost and Cooldown for you. You set Hot Lead, Target Account, Nurture and Disqualified.
  • Follow ups are tasks in Salesforce, created for you at the right moments. If an account you are working has no open task, you get "Add a next step for this account".
  • Your lists ("My Hot Leads", "My Target Accounts" and more) and a weekly dashboard by rep show what needs attention.
From the brief"I want fewer accounts getting more attention, a weekly answer to 'what did we do to break into this one,' and follow-up that doesn't live on someone's personal calendar."

2. Account statuses

Where: the status bar across the top of a prospect account page. Click a step to see what it means and the fields to fill. The same fields sit in the new Account Status Details section of the account page.

Status What it means Who sets it
Unworked Not being worked yet. The default, and what "New" used to be called. Default
Hot Lead We heard they might be shopping, or someone can get us in the door. You
Target Account A brand we decided to hunt this quarter. You
Working A contact is in an active Outreach sequence. Salesforce
In Opportunity An opportunity is open. Salesforce
Nurture They said not now. You
Cooldown We worked it and couldn't break in. Parked with a revisit date. Salesforce, when a sequence ends
Closed Lost The last deal was lost. Salesforce
Disqualified Not a fit. You, or Salesforce when a deal is disqualified
  • An open opportunity always shows In Opportunity, whatever the status was before.
  • Hot Lead, Target Account, Nurture and Disqualified stay as you set them, even while a contact is in a sequence.
  • Customer accounts work as they do today. Nothing changed for customers.
From the brief"In Opportunity: Set by automation when an opportunity opens and cleared when it closes. Nobody sets this by hand."

3. Hot Leads

Where: the status bar on the account, then Hot Lead Source in Account Status Details.

  1. Set the status to Hot Lead.
  2. Pick a Hot Lead Source (Dinner or event, Partner, Referral, Customer or market intel, Inbound signal, Other). Salesforce won't save the Hot Lead without one.
  3. You get a task, "Hot Lead: make the first move", due in 2 days.
  • A Hot Lead has a 60 day window. Hot Lead Days Left counts it down.
  • At 7 days left you get a reminder task. At 60 days you get a task to move it to Target Account or Nurture, or into a demo.
  • Hot Leads and Nurture accounts feed the Ad Audience - Hot Leads and Nurture report for LinkedIn ads and event invites.
From the call (Gina)"for the next sixty days, if somebody is a hot lead, we should be hammering them, knowing that they're in market shopping."

4. Target Accounts

Where: the status bar on the account. The dates show in Account Status Details.

  1. Set the status to Target Account.
  2. Salesforce fills Target Start Date (today) and Target End Date (end of the quarter).
  3. You get a task, "Target Account: first outreach", due in 3 business days.
  • Target Days Left counts down to quarter end.
  • On the end date you get a task to decide: book a demo, keep it for another quarter, or move it to Cooldown.
  • Target Accounts with no activity in 14 days, and any worked account with no open task, show on the weekly dashboard.
From the call (Gina)"Target accounts need to be worked ... every target account should have a task to it."

5. Working, Nurture and Cooldown

  • Working is set when a contact on the account starts an Outreach sequence. If the account has no open task, you get "Working: next follow up" (3 business days).
  • When the sequence ends, the account moves to Cooldown with a Revisit Date 90 days out and a revisit task.
  • Nurture: pick a Nurture Reason and set a Revisit Date. You get a task on that date.
  • You can change any Revisit Date. The task follows the new date.

Where: Revisit Date is in Account Status Details and on the Nurture and Cooldown steps of the status bar.

From the brief"The revisit date creates a task on that date, so nobody has to remember it."

6. Disqualifying an account

Where: the status bar, then Disqualified Reason in Account Status Details.

  • Set the status to Disqualified and pick a reason: Not ICP, Not a good fit, Not on Shopify, Direct to retail or Amazon, No subscription potential, Not decision maker, or Other.
  • You can disqualify an account without ever opening an opportunity.
  • When a deal is disqualified, Salesforce sets the account to Disqualified with the reason "Disqualified on opportunity".

7. Demos and lost deals

  • Opportunities start when a demo is booked. Before that, use Hot Lead or Target Account and a task, not an opportunity.
  • Demo booked: you get "Send demo follow-up email", due on the demo day. If the demo moves, the task moves with it.
  • Mark that task complete once you've sent the email. You then get "Follow up if no reply to demo follow-up", due 2 days later.
  • Closing a New Business deal as lost: fill Lost Follow Up Date with the date they asked you to come back (for example "August"). You get a follow up task on that date, or in 90 days if you leave it blank.

Where: Lost Follow Up Date is on the opportunity page, next to Closed Lost Reason.

From the call (Gina)"they want us to follow up in August ... And then I'll be like, do we follow up in August? And they're like, oh, I forgot."

8. Competitor contract end date

Where: Competitor Contract End Date in Account Status Details.

  • When a prospect tells you when their contract with their current provider ends, enter it here.
  • The date always shows the next renewal. Once it passes, it moves forward a year on its own.
  • 3 months before that date, the account owner gets a Competitor Renewal task to start outreach.
  • Two reports use it: Competitor Renewals Coming Up (the renewal tasks) and Ad Audience - Renewals Next 3 Months (for ads).
  • This is separate from the customer Contract End Date.
From the call (Gina)"I want to be able to just to know, like, July is the right time to talk to them."

9. Your views, reports and the weekly dashboard

Where: the Accounts or Opportunities tab, then the list view menu at the top left.

View Shows
My Hot Leads Your Hot Leads, with source, days open and days left
My Target Accounts Your targets, with days left in the quarter
My Working Accounts Your accounts in a sequence
My Nurture and Cooldown Accounts to revisit, with the revisit date
My Open Opportunities All your open deals
My Open Pre Qual (cleanup) Your Pre Qualification deals, for the cleanup
  • Today's tasks: the Tasks tab, "Today's Tasks" view.
  • Reports: the Reports tab, folder Cleanup Brief.
  • Weekly dashboard: the Dashboards tab, Weekly - Breaking In by Rep. It shows Hot Leads, Target Accounts, quiet targets, worked accounts with no open task, quiet Pre Qual deals and overdue tasks for each rep. Use it for the weekly pipeline call, separate from the open opportunity review.
From the brief"One dashboard by rep, so the call is about what we did to break in."
