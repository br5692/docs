Why we're doing this

Our market is finite. There are maybe 30,000 brands we could sell to, around 1,000 great ones, and about 500 customers today, of which roughly 100 are great. We've done a lot of enrichment in Salesforce, so we can sort by estimated revenue and see who matters.

What we can't do is see, at the account level, which brands we're actively hunting, which are warm right now, and which we've given up on. Pipeline review turns into a scroll through noise. I want fewer accounts getting more attention, a weekly answer to "what did we do to break into this one," and follow-up that doesn't live on someone's personal calendar.

Context
  • Revenue, Fit and segment are on every scored prospect
  • Play 1 to 5 reports already use them

Where it stands today

  • No status. 57,134 prospect accounts have no Account Status. Only about 3,000 prospects have one set.
  • Stale stamp. 612 prospects are stamped "In Opportunity," but only 55 open opportunities sit on accounts with that stamp. It's set by hand and it has drifted.
  • Missed stamp. 62 of the 117 open opportunities on prospect accounts belong to accounts that aren't stamped In Opportunity at all (blank, New, Working or Disqualified).
  • Prequal pile. About 80 of those 117 sit in Pre Qualification. We open an opportunity just to park an account, and it stays there.
  • Tier. Tier 1, 2 and 3 is the support tier for current customers, set by ARR with a few strategic exceptions. It says nothing about prospect fit. Segment (SMB, mid-market, enterprise) is a separate stamp and drives routing.
  • Tasks. We basically don't use them. Follow-ups go on personal calendars, often months out, and get missed.

Example: AS Beauty. Type says Prospect. Account Status and Tier are blank. There's an open Pre Qualification opportunity and three earlier Closed Lost ones. The only way to know it's in a sales cycle is to open the opportunity.

Customers do rely on Account Status today, so we are not touching how it works for customers.

Prospects don't rely on it, which is why we can clean it up.

Part 2: Tasks and follow-up

Anything that isn't fully in Discovery is not getting followed up on. Accounts sit in Prequal forever, and reps put reminders on their calendars three months out and miss them. I want a task in Salesforce for every account we're working, and I want reps held to breaking into a set number of accounts from their target list.

What I want the system to do

WhenWhat happens
AlwaysEvery account in Hot Lead, Target Account, Prequal, Nurture or Cooldown has an open task with a due date. Any that don't are flagged to the owner and in the weekly view.
Hot Lead is setA task is created for the owner, due in 2 days, to make the first move.
Target Account is setA first outreach task is created for the owner [due date to confirm].
Task is completedIf no reply has been logged, the next follow-up task creates itself one week later. If the rep logs a reply or meeting, they set the next step instead.
Nurture or CooldownThe revisit date creates a task on that date, so nobody has to remember it.
Prequal goes quietA Prequal account with no logged activity for 14 days, or no demo date after 30 days, is flagged to the owner to move it forward or to Nurture. [Thresholds proposed]
Many touches, no replyAfter a set number of automatic follow-ups, the owner is prompted to decide: keep going, Nurture, or Cooldown. [Number to set]

Tasks are the system of record for follow-up. Personal calendar reminders don't count.
