Steel Tide: skill tree v3 draft (98 nodes)

One continuous tree. Small nodes lead to Notables, Synergies (combos between your abilities), Forks (pick version A or B of an ability; the original stays equippable; one side owned at a time, free respec), Keystones (big trade-offs; one per path active), endless Mastery nodes (repeatable, rising cost, diminishing gain, no cap) and Core sockets (Cores drop from bosses and boost the nodes around them). Nothing is hard-capped. Tick what you approve, add a note to change one, then tap Copy my picks and paste it into the chat. Ticks are saved on this device.
