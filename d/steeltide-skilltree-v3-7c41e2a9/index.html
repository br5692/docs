Steel Tide skill tree v3 (draft layout, 98 nodes)

One continuous tree, center out. Tap a node to read it, approve it and leave a note. Small dots are stat nodes; bigger gold nodes are Notables; violet are Synergies (ability combos); blue diamonds are Forks (pick version A or B, the original stays equippable, one side owned at a time, free respec); red hexagons are Keystones (big trade-offs); the ringed node at each tip is an endless Mastery (repeatable, rising cost, diminishing gain, no cap); squares are Core sockets (Cores drop from bosses and boost the nodes around them). Nothing is hard-capped.
