Partner Portal UAT

The week of October 19. Work through the tests in order, in your own time. Each test lists its steps and what a pass looks like. UAT passes when every test passes and no launch-blocking issue is open.

Before you start

Partner portalfactory-partner-portal.pages.dev
Staff portalOn the portal's sign-in page, click Factory staff? Sign in here and sign in to the Factory sandbox.
Partner ReviewThe Partner Review app in the Factory sandbox, or Partner Review ↗ in the staff portal.
Your emailsUse your own work address with a tag for each test partner: you+uat1@factory.ai, you+uat2@factory.ai and so on. Every email arrives in your own inbox.
NamesStart every company and deal name with [TEST] and your initials, for example [TEST] JD Alpha.
First loadThe sandbox service can take up to a minute to wake. If a page is slow the first time, wait and reload.

Reporting a problem

Post in the project Slack channel with: your email, the time it happened, the page, the steps you took, what you expected and what you saw, and a screenshot if you have one. We reply in the same thread.

Launch blocking means a partner or staff task cannot be completed. Everything else is listed with an owner and a fix date.

1. Partner applications

1.1A company applies

  1. On the partner portal, click Apply to become a partner.
  2. Company name [TEST] JD Alpha, website factory.ai, Partner Type Global System Integrator (GSI), your name, email you+uat1@factory.ai. Submit.
  3. Open the confirmation email and click the link.
  4. Apply again with the same email address.
Passes when the link confirms the application, it appears in Partner Review under Applications > Pending, and the second application does not create a duplicate.

1.2Three more applications

  1. Apply and confirm three more companies the same way: [TEST] JD Bravo (uat2, Partner Type GSI), [TEST] JD Charlie (uat3) and [TEST] JD Delta (uat4). Use any example website, such as bravo-jd.example.com.
Passes when all three appear under Applications > Pending.

2. Staff decisions on applications

2.1Approve, deny and waitlist

  1. In Partner Review, approve Alpha. Open the email preview, then confirm.
  2. In the staff portal, open Partner Review ↗ and approve Bravo the same way.
  3. Deny Charlie with a reason. Waitlist Delta.
Passes when each decision shows the email before it sends, uat1 and uat2 get an approval email, uat3 gets the denial with your reason, and uat4 gets no email.

2.2Invite a partner

  1. In Partner Review > Applications, click Invite partner. Account [TEST] JD Echo, contact you+uat5@factory.ai. Leave Send the contract now unticked. Invite.
Passes when uat5 gets the invitation email and Echo shows as approved.

2.3A colleague is approved automatically

  1. In a private window, apply with your own plain work address, you@factory.ai (no tag), company name [TEST] JD Alpha and website factory.ai. Confirm the email.
Passes when you are approved without a staff decision, because your email domain matches Alpha's website. You use this address again in 5.1.

3. Partner agreement and sign-in

3.1Sign the agreement

  1. On the portal's sign-in page, type you+uat1@factory.ai and click Send sign-in link. Open the email and click the link.
  2. In Partner Review > Applications > Decided, find Alpha and click Send contract.
  3. Open the DocuSign email for uat1 and sign.
Passes when before signing, uat1 sees only the agreement; after signing, the status is Signed in Salesforce and uat1 reaches the dashboard.

3.2Links and sign out

  1. Click the same sign-in link from 3.1 again.
  2. Click Sign out, then open the portal's /dashboard address.
  3. For Bravo, open the Account in Salesforce and click Mark Contract Signed, so Bravo can register deals too.
Passes when the used link is refused and the dashboard asks you to sign in again.

4. Deal registration

4.1Register a deal

  1. Sign in as uat1. Click Register a deal.
  2. Customer [TEST] JD Harbor Coffee, domain harbor-jd.example.com, a contact name and email, deal name [TEST] JD Harbor rollout, value 50000, a close date and a role. Submit.
  3. Register a second deal, [TEST] JD Cedar pilot, for a new customer.
Passes when both deals show as Pending, a confirmation email arrives for each, and both appear in Partner Review > Deals > Pending.

4.2Duplicates and conflicts

  1. As uat1, register Harbor Coffee again with the same value.
  2. Sign in as uat2 (Bravo) in a private window and register Harbor Coffee with any value.
  3. In Partner Review > Deals, find Bravo's Harbor Coffee deal.
Passes when the duplicate from uat1 is refused, and Bravo's deal shows a conflict warning and cannot be approved.

5. Staff decisions on deals

5.1Ask for more information

  1. In the staff portal, under Most recent, click Needs info on Cedar pilot. Type a question and click Ask.
  2. As uat1, open the dashboard. Reply to the request in the portal.
  3. Sign in as you@factory.ai (the colleague from 2.3) and register [TEST] JD Willow order. In the staff portal, click Needs info on it and ask a question.
  4. Reply to that email from your inbox. Replies by email need the plain address, because your inbox sends from it.
Passes when each partner gets the question by email and in the To do list, each reply returns its deal to Pending, and both replies show in the deal history in Partner Review and in the portal.

5.2Approve and deny

  1. Approve Harbor rollout (uat1).
  2. Deny Cedar pilot with a reason.
Passes when approval creates an Opportunity on the Harbor Coffee Account linked to the deal, uat1 gets the approval email, and uat1 sees Cedar pilot as Denied with your reason.

6. Partner dashboard

6.1Tiles and list

  1. As uat1, open the dashboard. Hover each tile.
  2. Open each registration.
Passes when the tile counts match the deals you made, each tile explains itself on hover, and each registration shows its status, decision, reason and Opportunity stage.

7. Staff portal

7.1Most recent, Program health and Partner Review

  1. In the staff portal, look at Most recent.
  2. Open Program health. Hover a chart title.
  3. Open Partner Review ↗. Search for your initials. Then click ← Back to staff home.
Passes when your test deals and applications appear, Program health shows totals and charts including revenue by month, quarter and year, and Partner Review in the portal matches the app in Salesforce.

8. Droid

8.1Staff in Droid

  1. Add the portal to Droid:
    droid mcp add factory-partner-portal https://factory-partner-portal-tool-layer.onrender.com/mcp --type http
  2. Ask Droid "who am I in the partner portal?". Sign in to the Factory sandbox in the browser tab that opens and click Allow.
  3. Ask Droid to show the most recent deals, then to ask for more information on one of your test deals.
Passes when Droid shows your name, shows a preview before the request is sent, and sends it only after you confirm. The partner gets the email.

8.2A partner in Droid

  1. Add the portal a second time under another name, so the partner sign-in is separate from your staff one:
    droid mcp add factory-portal-partner https://factory-partner-portal-tool-layer.onrender.com/mcp --type http
  2. When the sign-in page opens, choose Factory partners, type you+uat1@factory.ai, open the emailed link and click Allow.
  3. Using that connection, ask Droid to show your dashboard, then to register a new deal.
Passes when Droid shows only uat1's records and the new deal appears in Partner Review > Deals > Pending.

9. Access

9.1Partners see only their own records

  1. As uat1, copy the address of the Harbor rollout page. Open it while signed in as uat2.
Passes when uat2 cannot see uat1's deal.

10. By eye

10.1Emails and phone

  1. Look through the portal emails you received during UAT.
  2. Open the portal on your phone, sign in as uat1 and register a deal.
Passes when the emails arrive in the inbox and read correctly, and the portal reads and works correctly on the phone.
