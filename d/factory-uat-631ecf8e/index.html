Partner Portal UAT

The week of October 19. Work through the tests in order, in your own time. Each test lists its steps and what a pass looks like. UAT passes when every test passes and no launch-blocking issue is open.

What you are testing

Partners apply to join Factory's partner program. Factory staff approve them. A partner signs the partner agreement, then registers deals for their customers. Staff approve the deals, ask for more information, or deny them. Partners follow everything on their dashboard, and both sides can also work through Droid.

You play both sides. Each test partner is you, with a different tagged email address.

TimeAbout 3 hours in total. Take a break after section 4.
You needA Factory sandbox user with Partner Review access (we set this up for you). Droid installed, for section 8. A browser that can open private windows, so you can be two partners at once.
SalesforceTo find a record, such as an Account or an Opportunity, type its name in the search bar at the top of Salesforce.

Reporting a problem

Post in the project Slack channel with: your email, the time it happened, the page, the steps you took, what you expected and what you saw, and a screenshot if you have one. We reply in the same thread.

Launch blocking means a partner or staff task cannot be completed. Everything else is listed with an owner and a fix date.

1. Partner applications

1.1A company applies

  1. Open the partner portal and click Apply to become a partner.
  2. Company name [TEST] JD Alpha, website alpha-jd.example.com, Partner Type Global System Integrator (GSI), your first and last name, email you+uat1@factory.ai. Submit.
  3. Open the confirmation email and click the link.
  4. Apply again with the same email address. Open the new confirmation email and click the link.
Passes when the link confirms the application, it appears in Partner Review under Applications > Pending, and the second link says "Your application is already with Factory."

1.2Three more applications

  1. Apply and confirm three more companies the same way: [TEST] JD Bravo (uat2, Partner Type GSI), [TEST] JD Charlie (uat3, any Partner Type) and [TEST] JD Delta (uat4, any Partner Type). Give each its own example website, such as bravo-jd.example.com.
Passes when all three appear under Applications > Pending.

2. Staff decisions on applications

2.1Approve, deny and waitlist

  1. In Partner Review, click Approve on Alpha's row, then Preview email to read the email. Click APPROVE, then Send email.
  2. In the staff portal, open Partner Review ↗ and approve Bravo the same way.
  3. Type a reason in Charlie's Decision note box, then click Deny and confirm. Click Waitlist on Delta, then WAITLIST to confirm.
Passes when each decision shows the email before it sends, uat1 and uat2 get an approval email, uat3 gets the denial email (the reason you type is saved on the record, not sent), and uat4 gets no email.

2.2Invite a partner

  1. In Partner Review > Applications, click Invite partner. Account [TEST] JD Echo, any example website, your first and last name, contact you+uat5@factory.ai and any Partner Type. Leave Send the contract now unticked. Click Invite partner.
Passes when uat5 gets the invitation email, and Echo is under Applications > Decided with Joined via Invitation and Status Approved.

2.3A colleague is approved automatically

One tester only. If someone on your team has already done 2.3, skip it and the email reply in 5.1.

  1. Apply as you+uat6@factory.ai: company [TEST] JD Foxtrot, website factory.ai (your email domain), any Partner Type. Confirm the email. If the page says "Your company is already an approved Factory partner, and you're signed in.", your email domain already belongs to an approved partner: skip the rest of 2.3 and the email reply in 5.1.
  2. In Partner Review, approve Foxtrot.
  3. In a private window, apply with your plain work address, you@factory.ai (no tag), company [TEST] JD Foxtrot and website factory.ai. Confirm the email.
Passes when your plain address is approved without a staff decision, because its domain matches Foxtrot's website. You use this address again in 5.1.

3. Partner agreement and sign-in

3.1Sign the agreement

  1. On the sign-in page, type you+uat1@factory.ai and click Send sign-in link. Open the email and click the link.
  2. In Partner Review > Applications > Decided, find Alpha and click Send contract. At "Send the partner agreement to <name> (<email>)?", click Send.
  3. Open the DocuSign email for uat1 and sign.
Passes when before signing, uat1 sees only the agreement; after signing, the status is Signed in Salesforce and uat1 reaches the dashboard.

3.2Links and sign out

  1. Click the same sign-in link from 3.1 again.
  2. Open https://factory-partner-portal.pages.dev/dashboard, click Sign out in the black bar at the top (after Register a deal), then open the same address again.
  3. In Salesforce, type [TEST] JD Bravo in the search box at the top and open the Account. Click ▼ at the right end of the row of buttons under the Account name (after Approve Partner Application). Choose Mark Contract Signed, not Send Partner Contract, then click Save.
Passes when the used link says "That link has expired or was already used.", the dashboard asks you to sign in again, and Bravo's Contract Status, in the Partner Portal section of the Account, reads Signed.

  2. Customer [TEST] JD Harbor Coffee, domain harbor-jd.example.com, a contact name and email, deal name [TEST] JD Harbor rollout, value 50000, a close date and a short description. Fill in the customer and click Next, the deal and click Next, then pick a Partner role and click Submit registration.

5. Staff decisions on deals

5.1Ask for more information

  1. In the staff portal, under Most recent, click Needs info on Cedar pilot. Type a question and click Ask.
  2. As uat1, open the dashboard. Reply to the request in the portal.
  3. If you did 2.3: in Salesforce, type [TEST] JD Foxtrot in the search box at the top and open the Account. Click ▼ at the right end of the row of buttons under the Account name. Choose Mark Contract Signed, then click Save.
  4. If you did 2.3: sign in as you@factory.ai (the colleague from 2.3) and register [TEST] JD Willow order. In the staff portal, click Needs info on it and ask a question.
  5. Reply to that email from your inbox. Replies by email need the plain address, because your inbox sends from it.
Passes when each partner gets the question by email and in the To do list, each reply returns its deal to Pending, and both replies show in the deal history in Partner Review and in the portal.

5.2Approve and deny

  1. In Partner Review > Deals > Pending, approve Harbor rollout (uat1), with the preview and Send email as in 2.1.
  2. Click Deny on Cedar pilot, type a reason in Message to the partner (it goes in the email), then click DENY to confirm.
Passes when approval creates an Opportunity on the Harbor Coffee Account linked to the deal, uat1 gets the approval email, and uat1 sees Cedar pilot as Denied with your reason.

5.3A conflict between partners

  1. In a private window, sign in as you+uat2@factory.ai (Bravo, the same Partner Type as Alpha).
  2. Register Harbor Coffee with the same deal name, [TEST] JD Harbor rollout, and any value.
  3. In Partner Review > Deals, find Bravo's Harbor rollout.
Passes when Bravo's row has an ⓘ next to the deal name that reads "Another partner of the same type is already approved on this deal." when you hover it, and Approve is greyed out.

7. Staff portal

7.1Most recent, Program health and Partner Review

  1. Open the staff portal and look at Most recent.
  2. Open Program health. Hover a revenue chart title, such as Attributed revenue by quarter.
  3. Click Partner Review ↗ (it opens in the same tab), click Decided, and type your initials in the search box. Then click ← Back to staff home.
Passes when your test deals that are still waiting appear in Most recent, Program health shows totals and charts including revenue by month, quarter and year, and Partner Review ↗ shows the same tabs and your same records as the Partner Review link from Before you start.

8. Droid

8.1Staff in Droid

  1. Add the portal to Droid:
    droid mcp add factory-partner-portal https://factory-partner-portal-tool-layer.onrender.com/mcp --type http
  2. Ask Droid "who am I in the partner portal?". In the browser tab that opens, under Factory staff click Sign in with Salesforce, sign in to the Factory sandbox if asked, then click Allow.
  3. Ask Droid to show the most recent deals, then to ask for more information on one of your test deals.
Passes when Droid answers with something like staff-ae:005…, which means you are signed in as staff (the part after the colon is your Salesforce user Id), shows a preview before the request is sent, and sends it only after you confirm. The partner gets the email.

8.2A partner in Droid

  1. Add the portal a second time under another name, so the partner sign-in is separate from your staff one:
    droid mcp add factory-portal-partner https://factory-partner-portal-tool-layer.onrender.com/mcp --type http
  2. When the sign-in page opens, under Factory partners, type you+uat1@factory.ai, open the emailed link and click Allow.
  3. Using that connection, ask Droid to show your dashboard, then to register a new deal.
Passes when Droid shows only uat1's records and the new deal appears in Partner Review > Deals > Pending.
